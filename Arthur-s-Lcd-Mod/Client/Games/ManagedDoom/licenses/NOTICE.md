Managed Doom engine sources in this directory are based on
[sinshu/managed-doom](https://github.com/sinshu/managed-doom) and retain their
GPL-2.0-or-later headers. The complete upstream license text is in
`LICENSE_ManagedDoom.txt`.

The upstream GLFW, OpenAL Soft, Silk.NET, TrippyGL, DrippyAL, MeltySynth, and
TimGM6mb dependencies and assets are not included in this integration, so their
license files are omitted. Doom sound effects and music are handled by the
Space Engineers audio bridge and the local `TinyMidiPlayer` implementation.
